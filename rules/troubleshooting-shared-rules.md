# Troubleshooting shared rules

These lessons apply to every troubleshooting record. They are reusable rules,
not client facts.

1. **Keep evidence with the claim.** A conclusion needs a source, a test, or a
   clearly named observation.
2. **"Passed" must be checkable.** Record what was checked, how it was
   checked, who checked it, and where the result can be reviewed. If any of
   that is missing, use `not checked`, `blocked`, or `needs review` instead.
3. **One change at a time.** Write down the problem, the proposed change, and
   the result before trying another change.
4. **Separate facts from guesses.** Mark an assumption as an assumption until
   a trusted source or repeatable check confirms it.
5. **Name the owner and next action.** An unresolved issue is not complete
   until someone and one concrete next step are named.
6. **Keep reusable material clean.** This repository may contain blank
   templates, fictional test data, rules, and documentation only. Never add
   client names, facts, exports, screenshots, credentials, or browser
   profiles.
